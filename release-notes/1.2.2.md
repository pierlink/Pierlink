Pierlink 1.2.2

终端不会再因为后台的屏幕副本出错而整个卡住；手机上看终端可以用按钮往上翻、往下翻。在程序里检查更新即可升级。

- 修复：终端偶尔整个卡住：打字、滚动、改窗口大小都没反应，点“接管”也没用，只能关掉重开（AI 终端里跑 Codex 时遇到过）。原因是后台给 AI 和手机读屏用的屏幕副本出错后，把整个终端拖停了；现在副本出错会自动重建，终端照常能用；万一后台出了别的意外，终端会显示已结束、可以重新连接，不会再光标照闪、看着像还活着。
- 新增：手机上看终端时，画面右边有往上翻、往下翻两个按钮，每按一次挪一小段。普通命令行里翻的是之前的输出；Codex、less、vim 这类全屏程序里，像在电脑上滚动滚轮那样，由程序自己往上翻、往下翻。

Pierlink 1.2.2 (English)

A terminal no longer freezes when the background screen copy hits an error; scroll a terminal on the phone with buttons. Update inside the app.

- Fixed: a terminal could freeze completely: typing, scrolling and resizing the window did nothing, taking over did not help, and it had to be closed (seen with Codex in an AI terminal). An error in the background screen copy that AIs and the phone read stopped the whole terminal; the copy is now rebuilt when that happens and the terminal keeps working. If anything else goes wrong in the background, the terminal now shows that it ended and can be reopened, instead of looking alive with a blinking cursor.
- New: when viewing a terminal on the phone, two buttons on the right scroll up and down a little at a time. At a command line they move through earlier output; in full-screen programs such as Codex, less or vim they work like the mouse wheel on the computer, so the program scrolls itself.
