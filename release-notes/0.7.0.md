Pierlink 0.7.0

- 手机连接：电脑不在身边时，用手机浏览器（iPhone、Android 都行，可以加到主屏幕）处理急事：
  - 回答 AI 的确认框（允许 / 拒绝），全部停止 AI；
  - 看服务器在线和 CPU、内存，同步任务状态，连着几个 AI；
  - 看电脑上开着的终端，在里面敲命令（有 Ctrl+C、Tab、方向键的按键栏）；危险命令要在手机上再确认一次；终端在等密码时手机不能输入，密码只能在电脑上输；
  - 看 AI 最近的操作记录。
- 设置 → 手机：打开手机入口，选监听地址（推荐 Tailscale；也可以用局域网），用手机扫二维码配对，电脑上点“批准”。只在选定的地址上开放，不对所有网卡、也不对公网开放。
- 每台手机单独给权限：查看（默认）、往终端输入、回答确认和全部停止；随时撤销，30 天不用的自动失效。
- 手机上的配对、输入、回答确认、全部停止都记在电脑上（数据目录 logs/mobile，保留 30 天），事后能查。

Pierlink 0.7.0 (English)

- Phone access: when you're away from the PC, handle urgent things from your phone's browser (iPhone or Android; add it to the home screen):
  - Answer AI confirmations (allow / deny) and stop all AI;
  - See which servers are online with CPU and memory, sync task status, and how many AIs are connected;
  - View terminals open on the PC and type commands (with a key bar for Ctrl+C, Tab and arrows); risky commands need a second confirmation on the phone; while a terminal waits for a password the phone can't type into it — passwords can only be typed on the PC;
  - See the AI's recent activity.
- Settings → Phone: turn on phone access, choose the listen address (Tailscale recommended; LAN also works), scan the QR code with the phone, and click Approve on the PC. Only the chosen address is opened — never all interfaces, never the internet.
- Permissions per phone: view (default), type into terminals, answer confirmations and stop all; revoke any time, and phones unused for 30 days expire automatically.
- Pairing, typing, answers and stop-all from phones are logged on the PC (data folder logs/mobile, kept for 30 days).
